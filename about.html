Our Story

How a shelf of borrowed books became a shop the whole neighbourhood reads from.

Ananya Rawat, Founder

A passion for books

Founded by book lover Ananya Rawat, Turning Leaves began with a simple mission: to share the joy and wonder of a good story. What started as a personal passion in the beautiful city of Dehradun has blossomed into a cherished local business.

Our philosophy is rooted in curation and care. We believe that every book has a story of its own and a reader waiting to discover it. We carefully select titles across genres and give our customers thoughtful recommendations to help them find their next favourite read.

From our humble beginnings, Turning Leaves has grown into a community hub for book lovers, dedicated to making Dehradun a more well-read, story-filled place, one book at a time.
